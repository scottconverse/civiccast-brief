One schedule for every city. Each city finds its week's meetings and writes each brief when it is due. The desk keeps running its own schedule while it is open; the Windows task below is what makes the weekly job run with the desk closed and after a reboot.

The schedule

The Windows task

One task, CivicCast Brief weekly, runs every city in turn every hour, and at sign-in. It runs as you, with no administrator rights and no stored password, so it only runs while you are signed in to Windows. It runs on battery too. After a reboot it runs at your next sign-in, and catches up a week that passed while the PC was off. When a city's job is already running, the task waits for the next hour rather than start a second copy.
