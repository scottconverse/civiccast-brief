Overview

The Parks, Open Space, Recreation and Trails (P.O.R.T.) advisory committee is made up of seven members, appointed by the Board of Trustees. They advise and provide recommendations to the Town staff regarding developing and utilizing parks and recreation amenities. This includes providing input on areas to be preserved as open space, establishing bikeways and trail systems, identifying new areas for parks, completing and updating the parks master plan,  current park upgrades, and suggesting options for park and open space utilization.

The current Board Liaison and one Staff Representative are as follows:

  • The Staff Representative for the P.O.R.T. Committee is Keith Knoll. Email Keith Knoll 
  • The Board Liaison for the P.O.R.T. Committee is Casey Grace. Email Casey Grace.

  1. P.O.R.T Master Plans

Berthoud Parks Master Plans

The master plan process created conceptual plans for Berthoud Town Park, Waggener Farm Park, Richardson Park, and Knievel Park. Plans for the parks examined the different recreational amenities located at each, including incorporating a recreation center on one site that houses future aquatics facilities, community event spaces, and facilities for youth sports activities. 

View the Parks Conceptual Designs
Read the Berthoud Parks Master Plans Summary to learn more about the master plan process and what is expected at each site.

2021 Open Space Master Plan

This Open Space Plan serves as the Town’s policy foundation to expand support for open space priorities and clarify the Town’s role and strategic actions. This Open Space Plan is closely aligned with the Comprehensive Plan to shape the physical form, health, and quality of life of the Town.  /documentcenter/view/4115/parks-master-plans 
Read the 2021 Open Space Master Plan

2022 Trails Master Plan 

The intention of the Berthoud Unified Trails Master Plan is to create trail connectivity throughout the Town of Berthoud. This trail system will also create connectivity of the town trail system with adjacent regional trails including the Colorado Front Range Trail (CFRT), BNSF Fort Collins/Berthoud Trail Corridor, Big Thompson River Trail, Little Thompson River Trail, Carter Lake / Horsetooth Corridor, and the U.S. 34 Non-Motorized Corridor.
Read the 2022 Trails Master Plan (PDF)

Berthoud Reservoir Master Plan

Establishing the Berthoud Reservoir Recreation Master Plan is the first step towards transforming the reservoir into a designated open space for the community, residents, and visitors. The Preferred Concept includes passive recreational amenities and improves the vegetation by restoring the native seed areas and through additional shrub and tree planting.
Read the Berthoud Reservoir Master Plan (PDF)



  1. Conceptual Designs - Future

Here, you will find the most updated version of the plans for upcoming amenities:

Berthoud Arboretum

Located at the southwest corner of Waggener Farm Park, the proposed Berthoud Arboretum will encompass 25 acres and include multiple walking paths, a water feature, a community hub, and space to reflect. 
Berthoud Arboretum Conceptual Design (PDF)

Outdoor Pool at the Berthoud Recreation Center

Located on the south side of the Berthoud Recreation Center, the conceptual outdoor pool design includes a zero-entry children’s pool and a second larger recreation pool, which includes lap lanes and a diving board. 
Outdoor Pool Conceptual Design (PDF)

Richardson Park (Mountain Vista Park)

Located north of Loveland Lake just west of Berthoud Parkway, this regional park will include up to six baseball/softball fields, a community event lawn, a destination playground, and a large community pool with slides, a lazy river, a play structure, and a resort-style pool.
