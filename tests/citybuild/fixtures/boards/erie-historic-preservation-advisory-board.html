Historic Preservation Advisory Board

Members

All members of the Historic Preservation Advisory Board are appointed as at large members by the Town Council. The first four members appointed to serve as initial members of the Board shall each be appointed for a four year term. The next three members appointed to serve as initial members of the Board shall each be appointed for a two year term. If you are interested in serving on the Historic Preservation Advisory Board, please contact the Town Clerk's Office.
